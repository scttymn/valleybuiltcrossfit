People usually message two or three gyms at once, and the one that replies first almost always gets them. After 15 minutes your odds drop off a cliff.

2. Call {{contact.phone}} about 10 minutes later if they haven’t replied. On the call:
• Say who you are and that you saw their inquiry about {{contact.interested_in}}

• Book it before you hang up, and text them the confirmation

Most people who join needed more than one message. Silence isn’t a no — it usually means they were busy. Keep it friendly and short.
